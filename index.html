Precision Medicine

From raw data to predictive care

Our healthcare digital twin pipeline converts heterogeneous clinical and multi-omic data into a living virtual model of the patient — then simulates outcomes before a single treatment is chosen.

  • Raw clinical data

    Genomics, labs, imaging, history and lifestyle captured in a governed, consented pipeline.

  • 3BIGS AI engine

    Multi-omic analysis, biomarker discovery and risk modelling at population scale.

  • Virtual simulation

    Treatment paths tested against a digital twin before touching the patient.

  • Predictive care

    Personalised, preventive protocols delivered through our clinical network.
