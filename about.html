Our Company

A Korea–Philippines healthcare innovation company

TrioHealix combines Korea's bio and AI science with the clinical expertise of Filipino physicians to create precision healthcare solutions for the region and beyond.

TrioHealix는 한국의 바이오·AI 과학기술과 필리핑 의사들의 의료 전문성을 결합하여 정밀 헤스케어 솔루션을 만드는 그로벤 헤스케어 헕신 기업입니다.

Who We Are

Three founders, two countries, one helix

TrioHealix was founded by one Korean bio-data scientist and two Filipino physicians who believed that the next generation of medicine would be built at the intersection of nations, not inside any single one.

  • Science with clinical accountability

    Research is only complete when it reaches a patient and improves an outcome.

  • Data with dignity

    Consent, governance and ethics govern every dataset we work with.

  • Access as the mission

    Precision health should not be a luxury available only in Seoul or Singapore.
